Changes to this grammar or generator require renewed inspection of this proof and `context::DEFINITION_ADMISSION`; admission for future grammars must be established separately.

The actual retained capacity is passed into parser admission. No statement is forwarded to a prior syntax parser.

Generated state/symbol limits are independent of the buffered malformed header length: no table path shifts more than five tokens, and any other order rejects without recovery. Temporary parser storage does not consume the semantic model-item counter; existing tiny-model definition/origin guards retain their prior outcomes. The source-derived buffer quota and work checkpoints bound even wide malformed headers before generated entry.
